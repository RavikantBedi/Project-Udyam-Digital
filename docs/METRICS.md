# Success Metrics: Baselines vs Targets

Every target in Project Udyam Digital is anchored in the empirical analysis of `smb_leads.csv`:

| Metric | Baseline (Synthetic Dataset) | Udyam Digital Target | Strategic Driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead → Activation Rate** | **7.4%** (368 / 5,000) | **12.0%** blended (15%+ on digital cohort) | Eliminating the 44% drop between Needs Assessed and Quote Accepted via instant transparent bundles |
| **Time to Activate** | **17.6 days** | **≤ 5 days** (≤ 15 min for digital stages) | Digital self-serve replaces 3-5 day field visit scheduling |
| **Cost per Activation (CAC)** | **₹15,000** blended | **≤ ₹8,000** blended | Shifting volume to Referral (₹4.1k) and WhatsApp/Digital channels while retaining partners for assisted closes |
| **Status-Chase Calls** | **3 calls** average per lead | **≤ 1 call** per customer | Real-time milestone tracker with automated WhatsApp status updates |
| **6-Month Churn** | **15.0%** blended (22.7% Micro) | **≤ 12.0%** blended (≤ 15.0% Micro) | Reducing chase calls prevents NPS drops; structured Starter contract protects margin |

### Full Dataset Funnel
- Total Leads: 5,000 (100%)
- Contacted: 3,406 (68.1%)
- Needs Assessed: 1,974 (58.0% of contacted)
- Quote Accepted: 861 (43.6% of assessed — **Critical Friction Point**)
- KYC Completed: 588 (68.3% of accepted)
- Activated: 368 (62.6% of KYC completed; 7.4% overall)
